To help you make the most of your time at RSS 2026, please review the important information below.

## Workshops — University of Technology Sydney

The RSS 2026 Workshops will take place across two days on **Monday 13 July** (half-day morning events) and **Friday 17 July** (full day).

**Address:** University of Technology Sydney — 15 Broadway, Ultimo NSW 2007

Please refer to the [Workshops at UTS]({{ site.baseurl }}/program/workshops/) page for detailed workshop times, building locations, room allocations and catering information. We recommend checking this page regularly for any updates.

**Catering:**

* **Monday:** Morning tea will be available
* **Friday:** Morning tea, lunch and afternoon tea will be available

At the conclusion of the workshops on Monday 13 July, please promptly make your own way to the Sydney ICC to collect your Conference name badge.

* **Address:** 14 Darling Drive, Sydney NSW 2000
* **Location:** Northern end of the precinct, closest to Cockle Bay and the Sofitel Sydney Darling Harbour
* **Walk:** The ICC is a 20-minute walk from UTS
* **Uber / Taxi Drop-off Point:** Iron Wharf Place (accessed off Darling Drive)
* **Transit Options:** Take the 501 bus from UTS at Thomas St after Wattle St to Harris St after Allen St, or the light rail from Paddy's Market to Convention

**Wi-Fi** — To connect to the UTS Wi-Fi:

1. Select the **UTS-Guest** network
2. Click on the "Attending a conference or event" link
3. Enter the access code: **Robotics2026**

## Full Conference — International Convention Centre (ICC) Sydney

### Venue

Robotics: Science and Systems 2026 will be held at the International Convention Centre Sydney (ICC).

* **Address:** 14 Darling Drive, Sydney NSW 2000
* **Location:** Northern end of the precinct, closest to Cockle Bay and the Sofitel Sydney Darling Harbour
* **Drop-off Point:** Iron Wharf Place (accessed off Darling Drive)
* **Transit:** Closest light rail stop is Convention; the closest train stations are Town Hall or Central (about a 10-minute walk)

### Name Badge Collection

Please ensure you have either a valid ID or your registration confirmation (with your name clearly visible) ready for presentation to collect your name badge. Name badges are to be worn at all times while at the Sydney ICC.

### RSS T-shirt Collection

RSS Conference T-shirts will be available for collection on **Tuesday 14 July**. Please ensure you present your t-shirt confirmation email and know which t-shirt size you ordered. Each delegate is entitled to one t-shirt.

### Wi-Fi

To connect to the Wi-Fi, select the **Robotics2026** network and enter the password: **Robotics2026**

### Conference Program

The full program is now available online and can be accessed on the [Conference Program page]({{ site.baseurl }}/program/overview/).

### Presenter Privacy

Please consider appropriateness and privacy before uploading anything to publicly accessible websites or social media.

### Catering

Catering will be provided in The Gallery (Exhibition Hall). There will be a dedicated station for dietary requirements — please speak to a catering staff member if you have any questions.

* **Monday:** Afternoon tea and dinner
* **Tuesday:** Morning tea, lunch, afternoon tea and dinner
* **Wednesday:** Morning tea, lunch, afternoon tea and dinner
* **Thursday:** Morning tea, lunch, afternoon tea and offsite Conference Banquet

### Conference Banquet

**Thursday 16 July | 6:30 PM – 10:30 PM | Luna Lu at Campbell's Stores**

The "Agibot" Conference Banquet will be held at Luna Lu at Campbell's Stores.

**Address:** Ground Level, Bays 4 and 5, 7–27 Circular Quay W, The Rocks NSW 2000

If you are attending the Conference Banquet, your ticket will be available with your name badge and must be presented upon entrance at the dinner. Please note delegates are to make their own way to the venue.

**Directions:**

* Train or light rail from Circular Quay to Town Hall, then a 10-minute walk from Town Hall to Luna Lu
* Drive is 12 minutes from the ICC to Luna Lu

### Family Friendly Room

For the duration of the Conference, **Business Suite 2.1** will be available as a family friendly space for delegates. The room will feature a live stream of the Conference sessions, along with access to a microwave, kettle and fridge.

Please see the team at the Registration Desk for directions to the room. Please note that this space is not supervised. Children under 12 years of age must always be accompanied by an adult.

### Getting to the Sydney ICC

**Onsite Parking** — If you are driving, we recommend parking in the International Convention Centre Car Park, which is connected to the centre, accessible 24 hours a day and monitored by security.

**Public Transport** — The closest light rail line to the ICC is the L1 Dulwich Hill line, serviced by two stops: **Exhibition** and **Convention**. The L1 Dulwich Hill light rail line departs regularly from Central. Alight at **Convention** for access to the Convention Centre, Darling Harbour Theatre, and the Grand Ballroom (about 65 metres from the station exit to the venue entrance). Alight at **Exhibition** for access to the ICC Sydney Theatre and Exhibition Centre (about 140 metres).
