## Megaron Athens International Conference Centre (MAICC)

**[Venue website](https://www.megaron.gr/)**

The MAICC is distinguished as one of the leading and most technologically advanced conference venues in Europe. A building of modern architecture dominates imposingly with its Doric austerity on the main avenue of Vasilissis Sofias.

Right in the heart of the city, only a few minutes away by foot from Syntagma square -the main square of the city- and easily accessible of transportation. The Metro station is located a few meters away from the main entrance of the MAICC. It is also very close to major hotels, museums, shops, and fine restaurants, many of which are within walking distance. In addition, it has **750** parking spaces, is just next to the Athens metro station « Megaro Moussikis» and is only **35** minutes from El. Venizelos Airport.

### The Megaron Halls

#### Christos Lamprakis Hall

The Christos Lamprakis Hall (formerly the Friends of Music Hall) is an amphitheater of excellent acoustics, amazing aesthetics with excellent visibility from all its luxurious seats. It consists of the square and the galleries, with large foyer spaces on both levels and is ideal for plenary sessions and presentations of high standards.

**At a glance**

- **Set-up:** amphitheater
- **Seating capacity:** 1,960 seats
- **Location:** 1st level – main building
- **Foyer space:** 1,780 m²

### Facilities for people with disabilities

Access to the Megaron for people with reduced mobility is easy, while all our rooms have specially designed spaces for wheelchairs.

The Megaron has received the **Helios Award** (in the category of public buildings) from the European Commission, in recognition of the facilities it provides for the disabled.

### Getting to Megaron

Megaron is situated at the corner of Vas. Sofias Avenue and Kokkali St.
This is a location most people know and you’ll find it easy to get to.
If you come by taxi, just tell the driver you want to reach Megaron. He won’t need any further directions!

You can also get here by public transport from any part of Athens:

- **By metro**, it’s just two minutes walking from the « Megaro Moussikis» Concert Metro Station to the door of the venue.
- **By bus**, buses Ε14, Α5 and 550 stop right in front of the venue
- **By electrically driven bus**, buses 10 and 3 stop right in front of the venue

Visitors to Athens can easily find Megaron:

- **From the airport** you can take bus Χ95, or a 35 minute ride on the Metro will bring you to the venue
- **From the railway station**, it’s just 30 minutes to the «Megaro Moussikis» Metro station
- **From Piraeus** it’s just a few minutes ride on the Metro.

### Accessibility

Megaron Athens – The Athens Concert Hall is fully accessible to visitors with reduced mobility. The venue provides step-free access, ramps and lifts, as well as specially designated spaces for wheelchair users in all halls. Accessible restrooms are available on all levels.

Participants requiring additional assistance are kindly advised to contact the organisers in advance so that the necessary arrangements can be made.

## Harokopio University of Athens

**[Venue website](https://www.hua.gr/)**

**[Useful information](https://interstudies.hua.gr/contact/)**

Founded in 1990, Harokopio University is a public higher education institution with a focus on education and research in areas including environmental sciences, geography, applied economics, health sciences, education, and digital technology. The University's main campus is located at 70 El. Venizelou Avenue, Kallithea.

The conference will use facilities in the following locations:

- **Main Campus** – 70 El. Venizelou Avenue, Kallithea
- **Library Building** – part of the University's main campus and home to the Library and Information Centre.
- **Informatics & Telematics Building** – 9 Omirou Street, Tavros. This building houses the Department of Informatics and Telematics and is located approximately 800 metres from the University's main campus.

### Getting to Harokopio University from Syntagma Square

#### By bus

From Syntagma Square, take bus 040 (Athens–Piraeus) and get off at the **Harokopou Station**. The stop is located close to the University's main campus on El. Venizelou Avenue.

#### By trolleybus

From Syntagma Square, you can also take trolleybus lines 1 or 5 and get off at the **Harokopou Station**.

#### By ISAP

From **Monastiraki** Station, take **Metro Line (Green Line)** towards Piraeus and get off at **Tavros Station**. From Tavros Station, continue on foot and the University Harokopio is approximately a 10-minute walk.

### Accessibility

Harokopio University is committed to providing an accessible and inclusive environment for all participants.

The **Main Campus** is fully accessible to people with disabilities and includes accessible facilities, elevators and accessible restrooms.

The **Library Building** is also accessible, with elevator access to the relevant floors and facilities for visitors with mobility difficulties.

The **Informatics & Telematics Building (Omirou 9 Tavros)** is also accessible and provides facilities to accommodate participants with mobility needs.

Participants who require specific accessibility arrangements or additional assistance are kindly encouraged to contact the conference organisers in advance, so that any necessary arrangements can be made to ensure a comfortable and accessible conference experience.
