The **Early Career Spotlight** is awarded in recognition of the outstanding accomplishments and exceptional potential of early-career researchers in robotics.
It includes an accompanying invited talk as a key part of the RSS program.  
These are researchers who are within a decade of completing their PhD (or equivalent degree), and no more than 7 years since their first faculty appointment.

Nominations are open for via this <a href="https://forms.gle/vU8UFRbt7o3np2cx6">form</a> until March 1st, 2026.

Previous awardees can be seen here: <a href="https://roboticsfoundation.org/awards/early-career-spotlight-award/">Early Career Spotlight Award</a>.
