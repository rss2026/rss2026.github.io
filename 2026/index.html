Robotics: Science and Systems
July 13–17, 2026
Sydney, Australia

RSS 2026 is complete. Thank you to everyone who joined us in Sydney for a wonderful week of robotics. Congratulations to our award finalists and winners!

News and Updates

  • Registration: Standard registration is now open through July 10. Early bird registration is closed
  • Keynotes & Early Career Spotlights: The speaker lineup and abstracts are now online.
  • Program: The program is posted

Call for Participation

We are pleased to announce the 22nd edition of the “Robotics: Science and Systems” (RSS) conference to be held at the University of Technology Sydney and the International Convention Centre, Sydney, Australia from July 13-17, 2026.

RSS has a long history of bringing together researchers in all areas of robotics from around the world for an engaging and focused week of single-track presentations, workshops, poster sessions, and tutorials.
