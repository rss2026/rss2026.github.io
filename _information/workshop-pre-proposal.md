The workshops proceed in two stages:

* **Part A** is the pre-proposal, due December 20, 2026.
* **Part B** is the full proposal, due April 8, 2027, and is completed only by organizers invited to submit after the pre-proposal stage.

All deadlines are 23:59 AoE.
